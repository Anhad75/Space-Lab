# AS75 Lab: 30-second demo video

**Format:** screen recording of https://anhad75.github.io/Space-Lab/ at 1920×1080, with voice-over.
**Tool:** OBS Studio or Xbox Game Bar (Win + Alt + R) on Windows.
**Before recording:** open the site in full screen (F11), zoom 100%, and close other tabs. Wait for the first load to finish so nothing shows "Computing…".

| Time | On screen (what to click) | Voice-over (read at a calm pace) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **#learn** tab, top of page. Scroll slowly to chapter 01 with **1.98°**. | "At the Moon's south pole, the Sun never climbs more than two degrees." |
| 4–10 s | **#plan** tab. Press **▶ Play** on the horizon view. The Sun skims the ridge. | "So a single ridge decides whether a lander has power, and Earth bobs on the horizon too." |
| 10–17 s | Point at the scenario strip: **A Viable**, **B Fails**, **C Needs relay**. Hover over the reasons. | "Slip the launch two weeks and the same site dies in a seventy-hour night. The backup site never sees Earth." |
| 17–23 s | **#sweep** tab. Tick **Highlight viable days only**, then click a green cell. The detail panel shows the same-day ranking. | "AS75 Lab scores every landing day for every site, so a planner finds the next good window in seconds." |
| 23–27 s | **#learn**, scroll to **07 Checked against reality** and the two scatter plots. | "And it's validated against JPL Horizons and NASA's own illumination maps." |
| 27–30 s | Back to the top of **#plan**, mouse still. Optional text overlay: *AS75 Lab · anhad75.github.io/Space-Lab*. | "AS75 Lab. Plan where, and when, to land." |

## Tips

- Rehearse the clicks twice before recording. Seven clicks in 30 seconds is tight but doable.
- If you go over 30 s, cut the 23–27 s validation shot first. The slides cover validation anyway.
- Speak over the recording afterwards rather than live. It's easier to time.
- Export as MP4, upload to YouTube (Unlisted) or Google Drive (anyone with link), and paste the link into the submission.

## Longer 2-minute version (if allowed or for judging)

1. **Hook (15 s):** chapter 01–02 of "Why it's hard": 1.98°, then 56% vs 0%.
2. **The planner (40 s):** play the panorama, then the telemetry readouts ("next loss of sun", "next Earthset"), then drag to rotate.
3. **The slip (25 s):** press **+14d** on scenario A and watch the verdict flip to **Fails** with its reason. Show the timeline.
4. **Date sweep (20 s):** viable-only highlight, then click a cell and "Open in Plan".
5. **Custom site (10 s):** Expand the map and click near Shackleton. The live horizon appears.
6. **Validation (10 s):** the scatter plots, plus Apollo 11 at 10.7° vs NASA's 10.8°.
